Support

Have a question, found a bug, or have an idea for Tides? Get in touch.

The easiest way is from the app: open Settings and tap Contact Support. Your email will include the app version, iOS version and device model, which helps with answering.

Something wrong with a puzzle? While playing it, tap the … button in the top corner and choose Report a problem. The email will include the puzzle number.

Or email justincamelcasedev@gmail.com with “TIDES: Support Request” in the subject.
